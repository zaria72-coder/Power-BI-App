About Power BI

Power BI is a business intelligence platform, which can provide organisations to turn data into reports, supporting data driven decisions. It interests me because I enjoy developing visualisations and dashboard to support my organisation in making effective data driven decisions. Click here to learn more about Power BI

Benefits of Power BI

  • Provides organisations with a report comprising data driven insights, enhancing performance.
  • Incorporates with other tools such as Excel, SQL, Azure, Microsoft Fabric and Sharepoint.
  • Enhances data security and governance with its role-based access control and row-level security.
